**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
Dayandi festival has the possible problem of either a very slow internet because of the crowd, or literal internet interruption for the entire day, this cause a problem for people that came in groups or family, since most people have different align of interest creating a chance for a split up or just loosing track of someone specically children.

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
By proposing a lightweight chat based mobile app that uses **bridge mesh networking** instead of relying on the internet, nearby devices can be use to rely messages between distanced users. and also have a qrcode generator & scanner system that can send message to anyone depending on the content of the qrcode.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
By enabling the user to communicate even without any internet, they can split up in groups and enjoy the festival more, and for the lost children they can just have an id tag (qrcode) that was generated by the app and when someone who found the child and scans the qrcode using the app they can message the parent to let them know their child is here.
